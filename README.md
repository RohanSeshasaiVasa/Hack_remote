Generally, many AC models do not have Wi-Fi models pre-installed and they cost so much for installation.
So thats why I have created this project where I can enble Wi-Fi options for AC's with not Wi-Fi settings.

We can just turn ON our Wi-Fi at home and let the IR transmitter face in the direction of the AC, and whenever we want we can turn ON and OFF.

Let's take an example that you created coming home from a hot day and you want to jump on the bed as sson as you reach home, but you need to turn ON the AC and it will like 5 to 10 minutes to get be cooled.
We can use this turn ON your AC when you are out but when you come home you feel the cool air. This is why I made this project
